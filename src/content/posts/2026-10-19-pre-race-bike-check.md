Race week is not the time to find out your bike has a problem. A short, calm check a few days before the start catches most of the things that end a day early.

## The checklist

1. **Tyres.** Look for cuts, embedded glass and thin tread. Pump them to the pressure you plan to race on, then check again the next morning for slow leaks.
2. **Brakes.** Squeeze each lever hard. You want firm resistance well before the lever reaches the bar. Spin each wheel and check the pads are not rubbing.
3. **Drivetrain.** Shift through every gear while pedalling. Skipping, grinding or a slow change usually means a worn chain, a dry chain or a cable that needs attention.
4. **Bolts.** Handlebars, stem, saddle and bottle cages should all feel solid. A quick check with a torque wrench takes a minute.
5. **Wheels.** Spin them and watch the rim. A wobble or a rub against the frame is worth a second opinion.

## When to book a service instead

If anything on that list feels off, do not wait for race morning. A workshop can only fix what it has time to see, and the week before a big event is busy for every shop.
