# Cam's Corner: how to publish a post

A one-page guide for Cameron. You do not need to touch the website code, and the shop does not need to be involved.

## 1. Log in

1. Open **https://app.pagescms.org** and sign in with the GitHub account you were invited with (check your email for the invitation).
2. Choose the **Dunkeld Cycles** project, then **Blog posts**.

## 2. Create a post

1. Click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title**: a clear, specific headline.
   - **Publish date**: today, or a date in the past or present.
   - **Short summary (for Google)**: one or two sentences, 150 to 160 characters. This is the text people see in Google results.
   - **Featured image**: upload a photo (see sizes below).
   - **Image description**: one sentence describing the photo. Required, and it helps Google and screen readers.
   - **Tags**: two or three keywords, such as racing, nutrition, bike care.
   - **Body**: write your article. Use headings to break it up.
3. Leave **Author** as Cameron MacNair.

## 3. Preview and publish

- Switch **Published** off to save a draft. Nothing appears on the website.
- When you are ready, switch **Published** on and click **Save**. The site rebuilds automatically and the post is live in about two minutes.
- To fix a typo later, open the post, edit it and save again.

## Formatting your post

- **Headings:** highlight a line of text and a small toolbar pops up. Use it to make the line a **Heading 2** for each section of your article, and a **Heading 3** for a sub-point under it. You can also start a line with  (for Heading 2) or  (for Heading 3).
- **Never use Heading 1.** Your post title is already the main heading on the page.
- **Writing in Word or Google Docs?** Use Heading 2 and Heading 3 styles, then paste the text into the Body field. The headings, bold text and lists carry across. Have a quick look afterwards and fix anything that did not come across.
- **Lists and bold:** highlight text to bold it, or turn lines into a bullet or numbered list from the same toolbar.
- The **Source** tab shows the post as plain text, if you ever need to fix something by hand.

## Image size

- **1600px wide, under 300 KB.** Large photos slow the page down and hurt your Google ranking.
- Free tools such as squoosh.app shrink a photo in seconds.
- Use your own photos where you can.

## SEO tips

- **One topic per post.** "How to taper for a 70.3" beats "Everything about racing".
- **A clear title** that matches what people would type into Google.
- **Aim for 600 words or more**, in short paragraphs.
- **Link to Dunkeld services** where it fits: a service, a bike fit or the workshop. Two or three links per post is plenty.
- Always write the image description and the short summary. Google reads both.
